About

Hands-on design, with the business experience to deliver it

I started as a designer and installer in 2005, creating 3D designs and passing exact measurements to installation teams. Since then I have worked through sales, design coordination and management, and since 2012 I have run DedotInternational Ltd.

That mix matters on a project. I can read an architect’s drawings, choose materials that fit the budget, and coordinate the people who build and fit the work, so what you approved in the design is what you get on site.
